Nightfall is designed to be frictionless. Whether you are connecting a SaaS application (like Slack or Zendesk) or building a custom integration using our Developer APIs, you can go from creating an account to discovering sensitive data in minutes.

Follow this end-to-end setup guide to deploy Nightfall.

## 1. Create Your Account

The first step is getting access to the Nightfall Dashboard, which acts as your command center for all policies, alerts, and integrations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Navigate to [app.nightfall.ai](https://app.nightfall.ai/) and click **Sign up**. Enter your required details and click **Create account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from `support@nightfall.ai`. Click the link to verify your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In">
    Once verified, click **Continue** to log in and open your blank Nightfall dashboard for the first time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Understand the Engine

Before connecting an app, it's helpful to understand how Nightfall identifies sensitive data. Nightfall relies on three core components:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Detectors" icon="radar">
    The base entities (e.g., Credit Card, SSN, API Key) that Nightfall knows how to find.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Detection Rules" icon="ruler">
    Logical groupings of Detectors (e.g., "Find Credit Cards AND require high confidence").
  </Card>
  <Card title="Policies" icon="shield-check">
    The final rule combining *What to look for* (Detection Rules) + *Where to look* (Scope) + *What to do* (Actions).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## 3. Connect an Integration

Integrations start inactive by default. To start scanning, you need to authorize Nightfall to access your environment. 

<Tip>
  If your required integration isn't available out-of-the-box, you can subscribe to **Firewall for AI** and build a custom integration using our [Developer APIs](/reference/developer-apis).
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your integration path">
    Navigate to the **Integrations** tab in the Nightfall Dashboard. Choose the application you want to protect (for example, Slack or Zendesk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Nightfall">
    Follow the specific authorization steps for your app. For example, in Zendesk, click **Begin Setup** &rarr; **Connect to Zendesk** &rarr; **Allow**, and install the marketplace app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Authorization">
    Verify the integration was successful. The status in your Nightfall dashboard should change to **Authorized** or **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. Deploy Your First Policy

With Nightfall connected to your environment, it's time to tell the engine what to look for and how to react.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate Detection Rules">
    Navigate to **Detection Engine** and use the Detection Wizard to generate your first Detection Rules. Once created, copy the Detection Rule UUIDs if you plan to use the API, or simply select them in the UI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Policy">
    Navigate to **Policies** and create a new policy. You will need to define:
    * **Scope**: Which channels, folders, or users to scan.
    * **Detection Rules**: Which rules you created in the previous step.
    * **Automated Actions**: What to do when data is found (e.g., delete, redact, or quarantine).
    * **Risk**: The severity level of the violation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Alerts">
    Set up notifications so your security team is aware of high-risk violations. You can route alerts to Slack, Jira, or a SIEM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 5. Test and Monitor

Your environment is now protected. Let's trigger a test to ensure everything is working correctly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a Test Detection">
    Input Nightfall-provided sample data (like a fake credit card number or API key) into your integrated application (e.g., send it in a test Slack channel or Zendesk ticket).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Violation">
    Navigate to the **Violations** or **Events** page in your Nightfall Dashboard. You should see your test violation populate immediately. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Metrics">
    Return to your dashboard overview to see your high-level security metrics aggregate in real-time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Congratulations!** You have successfully deployed Nightfall from end to end. Explore the [Cookbook](/cookbook/index) to discover advanced recipes and automation workflows.
